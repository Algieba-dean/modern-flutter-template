# Code of Conduct

This project expects professional, respectful collaboration.

## Expected Behavior

- Be direct, factual, and constructive.
- Assume good intent while still holding work to a high engineering standard.
- Discuss technical tradeoffs with evidence.
- Keep feedback focused on the work and its impact.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Repeated disruption of project work.
- Dismissing security, privacy, or safety concerns without review.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, block participants, or escalate
to organization administrators when behavior harms project safety or productivity.
